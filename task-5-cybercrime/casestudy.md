# Case Study: Phishing Attack on College Students

### What is Phishing?
Phishing is a type of cybercrime where attackers pose as legitimate institutions (like banks or universities) to trick individuals into providing sensitive data such as personally identifiable information, banking details, and passwords.

### Step-by-Step Scenario:
1. **The Hook:** A student receives an urgent email that looks exactly like the VIT Bhopal official portal, stating their "Semester Registration is Incomplete."
2. **The Lure:** The email contains a link that says "Click here to avoid registration cancellation."
3. **The Trap:** The link leads to a fake website that looks identical to the original portal.
4. **The Theft:** When the student enters their registration ID and password, the attacker captures these credentials in real-time.
5. **The Exploit:** The attacker uses these credentials to access the student's personal data or change their portal settings.

### Targets and Consequences:
College students are often targeted because they frequently use online portals and may not always check the URL carefully. The consequences include identity theft, loss of academic progress data, and unauthorized access to university resources.
