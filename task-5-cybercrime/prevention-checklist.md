# 'Stay Safe Online' Checklist for Students

Follow these 8 tips to protect yourself from cyber threats:

1. **Enable MFA:** Use Multi-Factor Authentication for all social media and email accounts.
2. **Check URLs:** Always look for "https://" and the padlock icon before entering login info.
3. **UPI Safety (Financial):** Never enter your UPI PIN while *receiving* money from anyone. 
4. **Secure Transactions (Financial):** Use official banking apps only and avoid clicking on SMS payment links.
5. **Password Hygiene:** Use strong, unique passwords for different platforms.
6. **Public Wi-Fi:** Avoid accessing bank accounts or sensitive portals on free campus/public Wi-Fi.
7. **Verify Senders:** Double-check the email address of any sender asking for urgent action.
8. **Report Phishing:** Immediately mark suspicious emails as spam and notify your IT cell.

### 🚨 Reporting Channels in India:
- **National Cyber Crime Portal:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/)
- **National Helpline Number:** **1930**
