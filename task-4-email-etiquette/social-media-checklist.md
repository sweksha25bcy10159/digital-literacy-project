# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

### ✅ Do's:
1. **Maintain Professionalism:** Treat your LinkedIn profile like a digital resume.
2. **Privacy Settings:** Regularly check who can see your personal posts.
3. **Fact-Check:** Verify information before sharing news or links.
4. **Networking:** Connect with industry professionals and alumni.
5. **Positive Digital Footprint:** Post content that reflects your skills.

### ❌ Don'ts:
1. **Don't Over-share:** Avoid posting sensitive personal info like address.
2. **Don't Use Offensive Language:** Avoid disrespectful comments or arguments.
3. **Don't Post Unprofessional Content:** Avoid photos that could harm job prospects.
4. **Don't Ignore Security:** Never click on suspicious links.
5. **Don't Tag Without Permission:** Respect your peers' privacy.
